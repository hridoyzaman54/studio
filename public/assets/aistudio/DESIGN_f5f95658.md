## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-fidelity, light-mode cinema aesthetic designed for discerning film curators, entertainment journalists, and ranking obsessives. It translates the reverence and atmospheric wonder of the silver screen into an immaculate daylight environment—reminiscent of architectural screening salons, archival print portfolios, and pristine gallery lounges.

### Core Character & Atmosphere
- **Architectural Alabaster & Quartz:** Void-like darkness is traded for crystalline, light-bathed mineral tiers. High-purity white planes and chilled quartz tints evoke timeless gallery architecture and tactile archival paper.
- **Deep Slate Calligraphy:** Text elements carry the decisive authority of crisp editorial print. Ultra-dense slate and deep charcoal replace pure pitch blacks, guaranteeing razor-sharp legibility without ocular fatigue.
- **Sunlit Amber & Film Guild Gold:** Strategic bursts of radiant amber and gilded metallic warmth pay homage to projector arc lamps, classical film trophies, and warm velvet auditoriums.
- **Glassmorphic Precision:** Semi-translucent frosted quartz layers, micro-beveled outlines, and luminous amber focus states produce an authentic sense of handling high-end physical media artifacts.

## Colors

The color palette shifts the theatrical experience from darkroom absorption to daylight cinema luxury, centering on quartz mineral surfaces, deep ink-slate typography, and projection-grade amber accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Surfaces & Mineral Tiers:**
  - `surface-quartz` (`#F8F9FA`): Canonical application canvas and global backdrop.
  - `surface-alabaster` (`#FFFFFF`): Elevated card canvases, dialogue sheets, and active input interiors.
  - `surface-muted` (`#F1F3F5`): Inset media trays, secondary tags, and track troughs.
  - `surface-glass` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)`): Backdrop-filtered structural headers and floating curation bars.
- **Accents & Guild Warmth:**
  - `primary` (`#D97706`): Canonical brand action, premiere badge accents, and primary interactive elements.
  - `secondary` (`#B45309`): Deep burnished amber for high-contrast active states, pressed controls, and critical status.
  - `gold-bright` (`#F59E0B`): Star ratings, podium-tier badges (#1 rank), and highlight glows.
  - `gold-light` (`#FEF3C7`): Soft tinted badge backdrops and warm highlight fills.
- **Legibility & Structural Delimitation:**
  - `text-primary` (`#0F172A`): Deepest slate for all headline scales, scores, and authoritative metadata.
  - `text-secondary` (`#1E293B`): Body text, critical analysis blurbs, and primary button labels.
  - `text-muted` (`#64748B`): Technical captions, film runtimes, and secondary categorization tags.
  - `border-subtle` (`#E2E8F0`): Hairline dividers and default card contours.
  - `border-strong` (`#CBD5E1`): Interactive item borders and active panel boundaries.

## Typography

Typographic treatments pair the architectural geometry of **Space Grotesk** with the neutral, engineering-grade clarity of **Geist**.

### Rules & Application
- **Editorial Presence:** Large titles and numerical rank markers are set in Space Grotesk. Negative letter-spacing maintains optical density against wide white backgrounds.
- **Tabular Figures:** Numbers in critical review tallies, runtimes, release years, and vote counts must strictly use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to preserve columnar alignment across vertical list rows.
- **Classification Badges:** All `label-sm` metadata chips apply full capitalization with expansive positive tracking (`+0.08em`) to reflect print archival classification tags.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts conform to a responsive 12-column grid system tuned for fluid cinematic artboards, ranked lists, and dense analytical statistics.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns with `margin-desktop` (3rem) and `gutter-lg` (2rem). Supports two-column splits featuring persistent film roll navigation and expanded review feeds.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns with `margin` (1.5rem) and `gutter` (1.5rem). Secondary meta-bars condense into expandable summary trays.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 fluid columns with `margin-mobile` (1rem) and `gutter-sm` (0.75rem). Card metadata reflows vertically, and interactive voting units dock to the card base.

### Layout Rhythms
- **Internal Component Spacing:** Primary listing cards use `space-md` on mobile and expand to `space-lg` on desktop viewports.
- **List Item Cadence:** Consecutive listing entries utilize uniform `space-md` gaps to present an uninterrupted ranking sequence with clear tactile separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted quartz transmission, crisp micro-borders, and diffused ambient warmth to elevate content above flat planes without muddy, harsh shadows.

### Atmospheric Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Foundation Canvas):** Solid Quartz (`#F8F9FA`). A pure neutral slate providing maximum contrast for poster keys and white cards.
- **Tier 1 (Pristine Cards):** Solid Alabaster (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` and subtle ambient drop shadow (`0 2px 8px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`). Applied to default list rows and content cards.
- **Tier 2 (Glassmorphic Sheen):** Semi-transparent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)`), backdrop blur of `16px`, bounded by a 1px hairline border of `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Outer shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`. Applied to sticky navbars, modal dialogues, and active floating controls.
- **Tier 3 (Gilded Accentuation):** Tier 1 or Tier 2 surfaces augmented with an inner top hairline highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 1)`) and an amber halo (`0 0 24px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.18)`). Reserved for the #1 ranked entry, active vote selection, and major CTAs.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp editorial precision with soft tactile corners.

### Implementation Principles
- **Base Components:** Standard listing cards, text inputs, poster frames, and dialogue containers use standard rounded styling (0.5rem / 8px).
- **Surface Overlays & Elevated Dialogs:** Modal windows, large film preview sheets, and hero ranking containers employ `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px).
- **Interactive Capsules:** Filter chips, vote action bars, and score badges deviate from structural containers to adopt full pill profiles (`9999px`), immediately distinguishing them as interactive units.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Amber:** Fill `#D97706`, label `#FFFFFF` in `label-lg`, subtle shadow `0 2px 10px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.28)`. On hover: `#B45309` with expanded 16px amber glow.
- **Secondary Quartz Ghost:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. On hover: background `#F1F3F5`, border `#D97706`.
- **Tertiary Utility:** Borderless transparent fill, text `#64748B`, transitioning to `#0F172A` on hover.

### Interactive Voting Pills
- Encased in a conjoined pill shell (`rounded-full`) resting on a `#F1F3F5` base with a 1px hairline border of `#E2E8F0`.
- Hairline slate divider between increment and decrement targets.
- **Active State:** Background shifts to `#FEF3C7`, the border illuminates to `#D97706`, and the numerical indicator transitions to `#B45309`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- **Default:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#64748B`, 1px border `#E2E8F0`.
- **Selected:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, 1px border `#D97706`.

### Ranked Cinema Cards
- Clean alabaster glass containers (`#FFFFFF` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) with 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Leading rank indicator set in Space Grotesk `rank-display`. Ranks 1 to 3 utilize amber-to-gold metallic text fills; subsequent ranks utilize `#334155`.
- Layout integrates a 2:3 or 16:9 poster image with subtle inset border, film title in `headline-sm`, director/year metadata in `body-sm` (`#64748B`), and a docked voting capsule.

### Input Fields & Search
- Inputs sit on pure white `#FFFFFF` with a crisp 1px border of `#CBD5E1`.
- Inset padding aligns with `space-md`.
- **Focus State:** 1px border transitions to `#D97706` alongside an outer ambient ring of `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.16)`.
- Placeholder text styled in Geist `body-md` using `#64748B`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base control uses `0.5rem` radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- **Inactive:** `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px `#CBD5E1` border.
- **Checked:** Core fills with `#D97706`, enclosing a pure white `#FFFFFF` glyph, backed by a warm amber micro-halo.
